Feasts and ale: a band with food stored past FEAST_KEEP 0.5 season hosts a neighbour and gives FEAST_SHARE 0.25 of the surplus (FEAST_MIN 0.5); prestige is earned in proportion to food given (PRESTIGE_GIVE 0.01 x food, x2 when ale is poured), also for food given to the hungry; the old share-of-spare-labour prestige term is gone. Ale = a sealed jar of grain at the water, brewed by grain farmers in warm enough summers. Compared with step 9 (docs/results-inv-step9-seeds1-10-500y.txt, run 40504455280) in sections 6-7; step 8b also in section 7. Step 9 did not measure prestige ~ size (the YearStats field is new in step 10), so section 6 shows step 10 values only for r and the top band share.
