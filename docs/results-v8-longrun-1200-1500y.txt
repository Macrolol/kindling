== Long-timescale drift
- No collapse and no runaway population. Every seed grows fast until about Y800-Y1000 and then
  levels off; seeds 2, 3, 4 and 6 have been flat for 3-6 centuries, seed 1 is nearly flat, and
  seeds 5 and 7 are still creeping up (+1-5% per century at the end).
- Disease roughly triples after farming spreads (about 0.1 to 0.31-0.41%/yr) and is now close to
  war as a cause of death. Together they take about 0.75-0.85%/yr at the end. This looks like the
  intended dense-farmer cost, but it is far above anything the 500-year runs showed.
- War creeps up to the top of the 0.25-0.45 target band: in the last century 4 of 8 seeds sit at
  0.43-0.46%/yr, and seed 2 peaked at 0.514 (Y701-800).
- Lineage dominance drifts: seeds 5 and 7 reach 66% and 64% and are still rising slowly at Y1500
  (seed 7 went from 20% at Y600 to 62%). Seed 8 jumped to 60% by Y300 and is slowly falling (53%).
  Mean max share is 46.6% against 27.4% in the 500-year runs.
- Crossings: the headline 6/8 overstates it. Five of the six are landfalls on very small
  landmasses close to shore that the map counts as continents. Large continents only 27-48 cells
  away (seed 4 #2, seed 7 #3) were never reached in 1500 years, even with sails from about Y600.
  Seed 8 reached a big continent 3 cells away in Y43, before its first boat (Y152), which looks
  like a start next to a narrow strait rather than a voyage, but it still breaks the "none before
  Y100" rule. Seed 8's Y956 landing on a 103k-cell continent 449 cells away is the one real
  long-distance crossing.
- Tech keeps moving but slows: iron and bronze reach all 8 seeds, sails appear everywhere after
  Y560, riding becomes almost universal. After about Y1000 there are very few new items (world
  item count 82-89 and flat), and several items are lost and re-found (outrigger canoe and skin
  boat lost in most seeds after Y1000, as better boats replace them).
